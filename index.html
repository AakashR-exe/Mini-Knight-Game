UMBRAL EDGE

⚔️ Blade Combos & Pogo

  • J or Z or Left Click 3-Hit Slash Combo
  • W + Slash Upward Slash (hits aerial targets)
  • S + Slash Down Pogo Strike (Air bounce resets jumps & dash!)

💨 Dash & Runic Spells

  • L or Shift Shadow Dash (Invincibility frames)
  • K or X or Right Click Aether Blast (Ranged spell)
  • C or H (Hold) Focus Heal (Recovers 1 Vitality Rune)

⏱️ 30-40s Duel Objectives

  • Watch boss telegraphs (flashing eyes & warning cues).
  • Pogo off charges and ground hazards to punish enemies.
  • Use the 75%, 40%, and 20% HP stagger windows for free combos.
